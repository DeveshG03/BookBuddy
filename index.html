Dear Sister,

Today is a little more special because I get to celebrate you — not only on your birthday, but also the bond we share as brother and sister.

We may fight, annoy each other and sometimes drive each other crazy, but underneath all of that, you will always have a brother who wants to see you happy and doing well.

On your birthday, I hope you get everything you deserve: happiness that stays, people who genuinely care about you, courage for every difficult day, and plenty of reasons to smile.

And on Raksha Bandhan, I want you to know that our bond means a lot to me. No matter where life takes us, you'll always be my sister and I'll always be cheering for you.

Happy Birthday & Happy Raksha Bandhan!
Keep smiling, keep dreaming, and never stop being you.

With love,
Your Brother ♡
